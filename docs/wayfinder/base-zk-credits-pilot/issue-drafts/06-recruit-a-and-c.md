Title: Recruit external operators A and C

Map stories: [Codex developer's first call](https://github.com/mangekyou-labs/haze-api/issues/25#codex-developers-first-call) and [x402 operator's own agent loop](https://github.com/mangekyou-labs/haze-api/issues/25#x402-operators-own-agent-loop)  
Sources: [B9 onboarding #12](https://github.com/mangekyou-labs/haze-api/issues/12), [B8 integration #11](https://github.com/mangekyou-labs/haze-api/issues/11)  
Blocked by: none  
Owner: founder; human-only

## Deliverable

Recruit two distinct, opt-in external operators: A, a Codex developer, and C,
an x402 operator who can integrate the project adapter into their own agent.
Schedule one separate 30-minute session with each and disclose the fixed $25
research honorarium, payable even if setup fails.

## Acceptance

- Both operators consent to usability research and have a scheduled session.
- A and C are different people and neither is part of the founding team.
- Record contact and scheduling information in private research materials,
  never in activation evidence or the spend plane.
- Explain that the honorarium is research compensation, not a product charge
  or evidence of payment intent.
